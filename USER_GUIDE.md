## 📊 **CENTER - Chart Area**

### Chart Header
Shows at-a-glance information:
- **Symbol**: Current forex pair (e.g., EUR/USD)
- **Price**: Current exchange rate
- **Change %**: Today's price movement
  - Green ▲ = Price up
  - Red ▼ = Price down
- **Signal Badge**: BUY 📈 / SELL 📉 / HOLD ⏸️

### Chart Features
**What you see:**
1. **Candlesticks**: Price action (OHLC data)
2. **Blue Line**: EMA 10 (short-term trend)
3. **Orange Line**: EMA 20 (long-term trend)
4. **Purple Bands**: Bollinger Bands (volatility)
5. **RSI Chart Below**: Momentum indicator

**Interactive Features:**
- **Zoom**: Scroll to zoom in/out
- **Pan**: Click and drag to move
- **Crosshair**: Hover for exact values
- **Toggle Indicators**: Show/hide BB and RSI

**Reading the Chart:**
- **EMA Crossover**: When blue crosses orange = signal change
- **Bollinger Bands**: Price touching upper/lower = potential reversal
- **RSI > 70**: Overbought (potential sell)
- **RSI < 30**: Oversold (potential buy)

## 📈 **RIGHT SIDEBAR - Analytics**

### 1. Signal Analysis Section
**Confidence Bar:**
- Shows signal reliability (0-100%)
- Green bar fills based on confidence
- Higher % = more indicators agree

**Strength Badge:**
- 🟢 **STRONG**: 3+ indicators align
- 🟡 **MODERATE**: 2 indicators align
- ⚪ **WEAK**: 1-2 indicators align

**Use Case:** Only trade STRONG signals with >70% confidence

---

### 2. Technical Indicators Section
**What you see:**
- **EMA 10 & 20**: Moving average values
- **RSI**: Current momentum (with status)
  - Red = Overbought
  - Green = Oversold
  - Gray = Neutral
- **MACD**: Trend strength
- **MACD Signal**: Trend direction

**How to use:**
1. Check if RSI is extreme (>70 or <30)
2. Confirm with MACD alignment
3. Look for EMA crossover
4. Make informed decision

---

### 3. Key Levels Section 🎯
**What it shows:**
- 🔴 **Resistance**: Price ceiling (hard to break above)
- 🟢 **Support**: Price floor (hard to break below)
- **BB Upper/Lower**: Volatility boundaries

**Trading Strategy:**
- **BUY**: Near support, target resistance
- **SELL**: Near resistance, target support
- **HOLD**: Between support and resistance

**Example:**
```
Resistance: 1.10500
Price:      1.09800  ← Current
Support:    1.09200

Strategy: BUY at 1.09800, Target 1.10500, Stop 1.09200
```

---

### 4. Forex Metrics Section 💰
**Understanding the Numbers:**

**Price Change in Pips:**
- Shows movement since last period
- Example: +15.3 pips = price moved up 15.3 pips
- Green = profit if you bought
- Red = loss if you bought

**Potential Pips:**
- Expected profit from current price to target
- BUY signal: Distance to resistance
- SELL signal: Distance to support
- Higher = better profit potential

**Spread:**
- Cost to enter trade (broker fee)
- Typical: 2 pips
- Lower = better

**Risk/Reward Ratio:**
- How much you can gain vs. lose
- 🟢 **>2:1** = Excellent trade
- 🟡 **1-2:1** = Acceptable
- 🔴 **<1:1** = Avoid (risk too high)

**Example:**
```
Risk: 50 pips to stop loss
Reward: 150 pips to target
Risk/Reward: 1:3 (Excellent! 🟢)
```

**Margin Required:**
- Money needed to open 1 standard lot
- With 1:100 leverage
- Important for position sizing

**Leverage:**
- Borrowing power (1:100 = control $100k with $1k)
- Higher leverage = higher risk

## ⚠️ **Important Trading Rules**

### Risk Management:
1. ✅ Only trade signals with >70% confidence
2. ✅ Prefer STRONG over MODERATE signals
3. ✅ Always check Risk/Reward ratio
4. ✅ Avoid trades with R:R < 1:1
5. ✅ Set stop loss at support/resistance
6. ✅ Take profit at target levels

### Signal Interpretation:
1. 🟢 **BUY + Strong + RSI <30** = Excellent buy
2. 🔴 **SELL + Strong + RSI >70** = Excellent sell
3. ⏸️ **HOLD** = Wait, don't force trades
4. 📊 **Moderate Signal** = Need confirmation
5. ⚪ **Weak Signal** = High risk, avoid

### Best Practices:
1. ✅ Check multiple timeframes for confluence
2. ✅ Wait for clear signals, be patient
3. ✅ Use watchlist for focus, avoid FOMO
4. ✅ Monitor auto-refresh for active pairs
5. ✅ Understand all metrics before trading
6. ✅ Never risk more than 2% per trade

- Disable and re-enable auto-refresh
