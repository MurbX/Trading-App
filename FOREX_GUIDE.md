# Forex Trading Quick Reference Guide

## 🎓 Understanding Forex Metrics in Your Dashboard

### 📊 **Pips (Percentage in Point)**
The smallest price movement in forex trading.

**Examples:**
- EUR/USD moves from 1.1000 to 1.1001 = **1 pip gain**
- USD/JPY moves from 110.00 to 110.01 = **1 pip gain**

**Why it matters:**
- Profit/Loss is measured in pips
- Your dashboard shows: **Price Change in Pips** (how much the price moved)
- Also shows: **Potential Pips** (how much you could gain from this signal)

**Calculation:**
- **For most pairs**: 1 pip = 0.0001
- **For JPY pairs**: 1 pip = 0.01

### 💰 **Margin & Leverage**

**Margin** = Money you need to open a trade
**Leverage** = How much bigger your position can be

**Example:**
- You want to trade $100,000 EUR/USD (1 standard lot)
- With 1:100 leverage, you only need **$1,000 margin**
- Your dashboard shows this as: "Margin Required: $1,000"

**Leverage Levels:**
- **1:50** = Need $2,000 to trade $100,000
- **1:100** = Need $1,000 to trade $100,000 ✅ (Your default)
- **1:500** = Need $200 to trade $100,000 (Higher risk!)

⚠️ **Important:** Higher leverage = Higher profits BUT also Higher losses!

### 📦 **Lot Sizes**

**Standard Lot** = 100,000 units of base currency

**Types of Lots:**
1. **Standard Lot**: 100,000 units ← Your dashboard uses this
2. **Mini Lot**: 10,000 units
3. **Micro Lot**: 1,000 units

**Example for EUR/USD:**
- 1 Standard Lot = €100,000
- 1 Mini Lot = €10,000
- 1 Micro Lot = €1,000

### 📈 **Spread**

**Spread** = Difference between Buy price (Ask) and Sell price (Bid)

**Example:**
- EUR/USD Ask: 1.1002
- EUR/USD Bid: 1.1000
- **Spread = 2 pips** ← This is your cost to enter!

**Your Dashboard:**
- Shows: "Spread: 2.0 pips"
- This is the cost you pay to the broker
- Lower spread = Lower trading cost

### 🎯 **Risk/Reward Ratio**

How much you can gain vs. how much you risk losing.

**Good Trades:**
- **3:1** = Excellent! Gain $300 for every $100 risked
- **2:1** = Good! Gain $200 for every $100 risked ✅
- **1:1** = Break-even ratio
- **Below 1:1** = Poor trade ❌

**Example from Dashboard:**
```
Current Price: 1.1000
Support (Stop Loss): 1.0950
Resistance (Take Profit): 1.1100

Risk = 1.1000 - 1.0950 = 50 pips
Reward = 1.1100 - 1.1000 = 100 pips
Risk/Reward = 100/50 = 2:1 ✅ Good trade!
```

## 🎨 **Dashboard Color Coding**

### Signal Badges
- 🟢 **Strong** = All indicators agree (high confidence)
- 🟡 **Moderate** = Most indicators agree
- ⚪ **Weak** = Indicators are mixed

### Price Changes
- 🟢 **Green** = Price going up
- 🔴 **Red** = Price going down

### RSI Status
- 🔴 **Overbought (>70)** = Price might drop soon
- 🟢 **Oversold (<30)** = Price might rise soon
- ⚪ **Neutral (30-70)** = No extreme condition

### Risk/Reward
- 🟢 **Green (>2:1)** = Excellent trade setup
- 🟡 **Yellow (1-2:1)** = Acceptable
- 🔴 **Red (<1:1)** = Not recommended

## 💡 **How to Read Your Trading Signals**

### 1. Check the Main Signal
```
📈 BUY / 📉 SELL / ⏸️ HOLD
```

### 2. Check Signal Strength
```
Strong = Trade with confidence
Moderate = Proceed with caution
Weak = Wait for better setup
```

### 3. Review Forex Metrics
```
✅ Potential Pips > 20 pips (Good profit potential)
✅ Risk/Reward > 2:1 (Good trade)
✅ Strong signal (High confidence)
```

### 4. Check Technical Indicators
```
RSI < 30 + BUY signal = Good entry ✅
RSI > 70 + SELL signal = Good entry ✅
MACD positive + BUY = Confirmation ✅
Price near Support + BUY = Good entry ✅
Price near Resistance + SELL = Good entry ✅
```

## ⚠️ **Risk Management Tips**

### Never Risk More Than 2% Per Trade
**Example:**
- Account Balance: $10,000
- Max Risk per Trade: $200 (2%)
- If 1 pip = $10, max risk = 20 pips

### Always Set Stop Loss
- Use the **Support level** for BUY trades
- Use the **Resistance level** for SELL trades

### Start Small
- Begin with **Micro Lots** (1,000 units)
- Move to Mini Lots after consistent profits
- Only use Standard Lots with large capital

### Use the Timeframe Selector
- **15min** = Day trading (quick trades)
- **1hour** = Swing trading (hours to days)
- **4hour** = Position trading (days)
- **Daily** = Long-term trading (weeks)

## 🚀 **Quick Trading Checklist**

Before entering a trade:
- [ ] Signal is BUY or SELL (not HOLD)
- [ ] Strength is Moderate or Strong
- [ ] Risk/Reward ratio > 1.5:1
- [ ] Potential pips > 15 pips
- [ ] RSI confirms the signal
- [ ] MACD histogram supports signal
- [ ] Price action respects support/resistance
- [ ] You understand the margin required
- [ ] Stop loss and take profit levels are set

## 📚 **Common Forex Terms**

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Base Currency** | First currency in pair (EUR in EUR/USD) |
| **Quote Currency** | Second currency in pair (USD in EUR/USD) |
| **Long** | Buying a currency pair |
| **Short** | Selling a currency pair |
| **Bullish** | Expecting price to rise |
| **Bearish** | Expecting price to fall |
| **Volatility** | How much price moves (Bollinger Bands show this) |
| **Liquidity** | How easy to buy/sell (Major pairs have high liquidity) |

## 🔥 **Pro Tips**

1. **Trade Major Pairs First**
   - EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY have lowest spreads
   - More predictable and liquid

2. **Avoid Trading During News**
   - Price can move 100+ pips in seconds
   - Your stop loss might not protect you

3. **Use Multiple Timeframes**
   - Check Daily for overall trend
   - Use 1hour for entry timing
   - Confirm with 15min for precision

4. **Follow the Trend**
   - "Trend is your friend"
   - Don't fight strong trends
   - If Daily shows BUY, look for BUY setups on lower timeframes

5. **Keep a Trading Journal**
   - Record every trade
   - Note what worked and what didn't
   - Learn from your mistakes
